# Shared material density and unit-aware stock transfers

The material now holds a verified g/mL density shared by its department links. Department stock units remain independent. Future BOM/source calculations use that density; adopted production requirements and reservations retain their captured density so later master edits do not change approved issue quantities. Allocation, forecasts, issue validation and repair comparisons convert physical stock using the adopted measurement.

Internal transfer preview and issue support mass/volume conversions with shared density, protect other reservations and retain the lot's original physical unit. Issue snapshots source quantity, requested quantity, receiving quantity/unit and density. Receipt validates destination ownership/shelves, exact whole-lot receiving quantity, availability and current grants. The saved conversion is used even if density later changes. Split shelving assigns any decimal remainder to the last shelf and preserves the exact source physical quantity. Repeated receipt is refused, and multi-lot holdings remain pending until every lot is received. Physical ledgers keep the original lot unit; holding DTOs expose receiving quantity/unit to the desktop.

Apply `AddSharedMaterialDensity` before the companion desktop. It backfills only unambiguous positive measurements explicitly tagged with an active g/mL unit. Conflicting or untagged measurements are not guessed. Legacy department measurements remain readable until a shared verified measurement is supplied. Every subsequent shared-density edit records old/new values, actor and time in append-only history, protected by EF and PostgreSQL triggers.

Permissions: new `CanEditRawMaterialDensity` / `CanEditPackagingMaterialDensity`, registered once under Warehouse / Linked Materials. The migration grants them with matching access types to roles holding `CanLinkRawMaterials` / `CanLinkPackagingMaterials` respectively, preserving existing density-edit access. POST `/api/v1/material/department` retains its material-kind link policy and checks the density key separately against the stored material kind before writes. Stock-only edits omit the density pair. Issue and receipt retain `CanIssueRawMaterialStockTransfers` / `CanIssuePackagingMaterialStockTransfers`; department ownership and current grants are checked inside the stock transaction. No live grant changes or stock operations are performed by this implementation work.

Companion desktop branch: `codex/material-density-transfers`. Optional read fields expose shared density, physical source quantity and saved receiving quantities. The frontend must use the receiving quantity/unit for shelving and retain the existing request unit for issuing a transfer. Missing verified density refuses a cross-dimension transfer; quantities are never treated as interchangeable 1:1.

Validation includes raw/packaging density deny/allow, L↔kg transfer/receipt totals and duplicate receipt, production reservation/issue snapshots, legacy density preservation, existing BOM conversion and real PostgreSQL migration/grant/audit and transfer concurrency checks. No pending EF model changes.

This PR includes the extra-material availability fix from companion PR #1893 until that commit reaches the target branch. Extra-material issue uses requested units for validation, then deducts and audits the converted physical quantity in the lot unit. Integration regressions cover both raw and packaging extra-material issues after L/kg conversion.

## Supplying store's unit (2026-11-18)

A transfer is requested in the receiving department's unit and stored unchanged. The in-bound list and `GET stock-transfer/{id}` add optional `supplierQuantity` / `supplierUoM`: the same quantity in the supplying department's linked stock unit, converted with the shared density (20,000 L reads 20,720 kg at 1.036 g/mL). They are null when the supplier has no single link or the units cannot be converted. A shortfall is reported in the supplier's unit, with the requested figure in its largest whole unit ("The supplying store has 3,743.3 kg free of the 20,720 kg needed for 20,000 L. It is short by 16,976.7 kg."); before this it printed the raw request-unit difference without a unit (for example 16386776.06… for an mL request).

One stock rule for every screen (`StoreStock`), the allocator's: on hand is everything on the store's shelves; expired is lots past expiry/retest; unavailable is in-date lots not released (quarantine, frozen) or whose shelf and lot units disagree; usable is the rest; reserved is live holds on usable shelves, counted at most up to the shelf, including older holds with no shelf recorded; free is usable less reserved. A live hold (`ProductionMaterialAllocator.UnissuedReservations`) is not deleted, not released (Unstart puts it back on the shelf), not for a cancelled batch and not yet issued to the production floor. Screens on this rule: approved materials (all three lists), the schedule forecast (adds optional `unavailableQuantity`, `usableQuantity`, `freeQuantity`), stock by warehouse/department/across warehouses, `{materialId}/stock/{warehouseId}`, departments with enough stock, stock level, the production step material view, the reserved tab, reserved and reserved-batch reports, warehouse stock summary and batch detail (department from the store, not the material link), below-minimum and reorder-vs-stock (per department's own store, free stock). Before this, these mixed a movement ledger, all lots regardless of status, and holds already issued or released, which is why reserved could exceed stock. `deploy/repairs/diagnose-material-holds.sql` lists one material's holds with whether each is live.

Stock requisitions in `approval/my-pending` carry `batchNumber` and `productName` of their production batch.
